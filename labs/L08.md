# Lab 8: Digital Elevation Model (DEM) Analysis: Slope, Aspect & Contours

**Course Code:** CE452 | **Credits:** 2 | **Week:** 8  
**Estimated Time:** 120 Minutes | **Mapped Outcome:** CO4 (L4, L5)  
**Primary Tools:** QGIS Raster Analysis, GDAL DEM Tools, CartoDEM / SRTM

---

## 🎯 Aim & Objectives
1. Understand Digital Elevation Models (DEM) structure, cell resolution, and vertical accuracy.
2. Ingest 30 m CartoDEM (from ISRO Bhuvan) or SRTM 1 Arc-Second DEM (from USGS EarthExplorer).
3. Compute morphometric derivatives: **Slope (Degrees and %)**, **Aspect (Sun exposure direction)**, and **Hillshade (3D relief visualization)**.
4. Extract vector **Elevation Contours** at 10-meter intervals and label index contours.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Hands-On Procedure

### Phase 1: DEM Sourcing & Reprojection
:::steps
1. Download a 30m DEM tile covering your study catchment from ISRO Bhuvan (CartoDEM) or USGS EarthExplorer.
2. Load the GeoTIFF raster into QGIS (`Raster → Add Layer → Add Raster Layer...`).
3. Warp/Reproject the DEM into metric UTM (`EPSG:32643` or `EPSG:32645`) so horizontal and vertical units are both in meters.
:::

### Phase 2: Morphometric Derivations
:::steps
1. **Hillshade Calculation (3D Shaded Relief)**:
   - Go to **Raster $\rightarrow$ Analysis $\rightarrow$ Hillshade...**.
   - Azimuth of light: `315°` (North-West sun angle standard).
   - Sun elevation: `45°`.
   - Output: `Hillshade.tif`.
   - In Layer properties, set Blending mode to `Multiply` over the colored DEM for photorealistic 3D terrain rendering!
2. **Slope Calculation**:
   - Go to **Raster $\rightarrow$ Analysis $\rightarrow$ Slope...**.
   - Check option to compute slope as **Percent** (for civil engineering drainage standards) or **Degrees**.
   - Output: `Slope_pct.tif`.
3. **Aspect Calculation**:
   - Go to **Raster $\rightarrow$ Analysis $\rightarrow$ Aspect...**.
   - Generates compass direction (0° to 360°) of down-slope facing slopes.
:::

### Phase 3: Contour Line Extraction
:::steps
1. Navigate to **Raster $\rightarrow$ Extraction $\rightarrow$ Contour...**.
2. Set:
   - Interval between contour lines: `10.0 meters`
   - Attribute name: `ELEV`
   - Output: `Contours_10m.shp`
3. In Layer Styling $\rightarrow$ Labels, add label expression `ELEV || ' m'` with a subtle buffer halo so elevation numbers read cleanly along contour lines.
:::
