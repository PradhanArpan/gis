# Lab 14: Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (AHP-GIS) for Site Suitability

**Course Code:** CE452 | **Credits:** 2 | **Week:** 14  
**Estimated Time:** 120 Minutes | **Mapped Outcome:** CO3, CO4 (L4, L5)  
**Primary Tools:** QGIS Raster Calculator, Reclassify by Table, AHP Weighting

---

## 🎯 Aim & Objectives
1. Apply **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)** to solve engineering siting challenges (e.g. Municipal Solid Waste Landfill or Rainwater Harvesting Reservoir Siting).
2. Calculate criterion weights using **Saaty's Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP)** pairwise comparison matrix.
3. Standardize diverse physical raster criteria into a normalized suitability scale (1 = Least Suitable, 5 = Highly Suitable).
4. Perform **Weighted Linear Combination (WLC)** raster overlay modeling.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Hands-On Procedure

### Phase 1: Criteria Selection & Exclusion Buffers
For a sanitary landfill siting study, identify environmental and engineering criteria:
- **Slope**: Gentle slopes (< 5%) are preferred; steep slopes (> 15%) are rejected.
- **Distance to Water Bodies**: Must be $> 500\text{ m}$ away (environmental protection buffer).
- **Distance to Road Network**: Must be within $200\text{ m} - 1000\text{ m}$ for accessibility.
- **Soil Permeability / Land Cover**: Low permeability clay/barren land preferred over fertile agricultural soil.

### Phase 2: Raster Reclassification & Normalization
:::steps
1. In Processing Toolbox, search for **Reclassify by table** (or Raster Calculator).
2. For each input raster, convert physical values into suitability ratings (Scale 1 to 5):
   - Slope $0-5\% \rightarrow 5$; $5-10\% \rightarrow 4$; $10-15\% \rightarrow 2$; $> 15\% \rightarrow 1$.
   - Distance to river $< 500\text{ m} \rightarrow 0$ (absolute exclusion constraint); $> 500\text{ m} \rightarrow 5$.
3. Output standardized criterion grids: `Slope_Score.tif`, `River_Score.tif`, `Road_Score.tif`, `LULC_Score.tif`.
:::

### Phase 3: Weighted Overlay in Raster Calculator
:::steps
1. Using AHP, assign normalized criterion weights (summing to 1.0):
   - $W_{\text{river}} = 0.40$
   - $W_{\text{slope}} = 0.25$
   - $W_{\text{lulc}} = 0.20$
   - $W_{\text{road}} = 0.15$
2. In **Raster Calculator**, evaluate the composite suitability equation:
   ```sql
   (0.40 * "River_Score@1") + (0.25 * "Slope_Score@1") + (0.20 * "LULC_Score@1") + (0.15 * "Road_Score@1")
   ```
3. Output: `Landfill_Suitability_Index.tif`.
4. Style with a Red-Yellow-Green ramp to highlight optimal candidate development sites.
:::
