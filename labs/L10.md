## 🎯 Aim & Objectives
1. Understand the electromagnetic spectrum (EMS), spectral bands, and spatial vs spectral resolution.
2. Ingest raw 10m multispectral Level-2A (L2A Bottom-Of-Atmosphere reflectance) imagery downloaded from the **Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE)**.
3. Construct composite multiband rasters:
   - **True Color Composite (TCC)**: Red (B4) - Green (B3) - Blue (B2)
   - **Standard False Color Composite (Standard FCC)**: NIR (B8) - Red (B4) - Green (B3)
   - **Agriculture / Moisture Composite**: SWIR-1 (B11) - NIR (B8) - Blue (B2)
4. Interpret land cover signatures: why dense vegetation appears bright red in NIR-based FCC.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Hands-On Procedure

### Phase 1: Sentinel-2 Band Identification
Sentinel-2 MSI (Multi-Spectral Instrument) provides 13 spectral bands:
- **Band 2 (Blue - 490 nm)**: 10 m resolution (water body penetration, smoke/haze discrimination)
- **Band 3 (Green - 560 nm)**: 10 m resolution (peak vegetation greenness)
- **Band 4 (Red - 665 nm)**: 10 m resolution (chlorophyll absorption)
- **Band 8 (Near-Infrared / NIR - 842 nm)**: 10 m resolution (high vegetation cell structure reflectance)
- **Band 11 (Shortwave-Infrared / SWIR - 1610 nm)**: 20 m resolution (moisture content, built-up surfaces)

### Phase 2: Building Virtual Raster (VRT) Stacks
:::steps
1. In QGIS, navigate to **Raster $\rightarrow$ Miscellaneous $\rightarrow$ Build Virtual Raster...**.
2. Click **Input layers** (`...`) and select individual Sentinel-2 band GeoTIFFs: `B02.jp2`, `B03.jp2`, `B04.jp2`, `B08.jp2`.
3. Check $\checkmark$ **Place each input file into a separate band** (Crucial step!).
4. Save file as `Sentinel2_Stacked.vrt`. Click **Run**.
:::

### Phase 3: Symbology & False Color Composites
:::steps
1. Double-click `Sentinel2_Stacked.vrt` in Layers panel $\rightarrow$ **Symbology**.
2. Render type: **Multiband color**.
3. **True Color Composite (Natural Eye View)**:
   - Red band: `Band 3 (B04 - Red)`
   - Green band: `Band 2 (B03 - Green)`
   - Blue band: `Band 1 (B02 - Blue)`
   - Contrast enhancement: `Stretch to MinMax` (Cumulative count cut 2% - 98%). Click **Apply**.
4. **Standard False Color Composite (FCC)**:
   - Red band: `Band 4 (B08 - NIR)`
   - Green band: `Band 3 (B04 - Red)`
   - Blue band: `Band 2 (B03 - Green)`
   - Click **Apply**: Dense healthy crop cover and forest canopy glow in bright scarlet red, while urban concrete appears cyan/slate grey, and clear water absorbs all NIR appearing jet black!
:::
