# Assessment Scheme & Lab Evaluation Rubrics

**Course Code:** CE452 | **Title:** Geographic Information Systems Laboratory  
**Evaluation Model:** Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA: 70 Marks) | End Semester Examination (ESE: 30 Marks) | Total: 100 Marks

---

## 📊 Evaluation Breakdown

| Component | Weightage | Description |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Weekly Practical Performance (Day-to-day work)** | 30 Marks | Attendance, timely completion of weekly lab exercises, active problem solving, and error-free geoprocessing outputs. |
| **Laboratory Fieldbook & Report Submissions** | 20 Marks | Neatly formatted digital lab reports, verified screenshots, GCP tables, and metadata citations. |
| **Model Practical Examination (CIA II)** | 20 Marks | Timed 2-hour hands-on examination evaluating independent georeferencing, digitization, and spatial analysis competency. |
| **End Semester Practical Examination & Viva** | 30 Marks | Comprehensive hands-on test + individual technical viva voce on GIS theory, error analysis, and project defense. |

---

## 🎯 Rubric for Weekly Lab Submissions

| Performance Level | Marks (per lab) | Criteria |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Exemplary (L4/L5)** | 9–10 | Flawless geometric accuracy (RMS error $< 0.5$ px), complete topological consistency, clean symbology, correct projection assignment, and clear interpretation of results. |
| **Proficient (L3)** | 7–8 | Minor residual error ($< 1.0$ px), attributes properly populated, standard layout elements present, results correctly derived. |
| **Developing (L2)** | 5–6 | Coordinates partially distorted, minor topological gaps or overlapping slivers, missing cartographic elements. |
| **Unacceptable (L1)** | $< 5$ | Unreferenced data, swapped X/Y axes, unprojected metric measurements, or incomplete submission. |
