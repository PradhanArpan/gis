# Capstone GIS Project & Problem-Based Learning Guide

## Driving Objective
Apply modern Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and remote sensing principles to solve a real-world civil engineering, environmental, or water resources problem within an adopted Indian watershed, municipal ward, or infrastructure corridor.

---

## 🏛️ Project Themes & Approved Study Areas

Student batches (4–5 students) choose one specific project theme:
1. **Urban Flood Inundation & Micro-Watershed Drainage Assessment**: Delineate urban sub-catchments, identify low-lying depressions from DEM, and map flood-vulnerable structures.
2. **Solid Waste Disposal / Landfill Siting via AHP-GIS**: Combine slope, groundwater depth, road accessibility, and environmental setback constraints using Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis.
3. **Rainwater Harvesting & Artificial Recharge Potential**: Map rooftop catchments, soil infiltration capacity, and identify optimal locations for percolation tanks or recharge shafts.
4. **Land Use / Land Cover (LULC) Decadal Change Detection**: Compare historical Landsat imagery with modern Sentinel-2 data to quantify urban sprawl and wetland loss.
5. **Transportation Road Corridor Alignment & Cut/Fill Volumetrics**: Digitize proposed bypass alignments, extract terrain profiles from 30 m DEM, and evaluate earthwork volumes.

---

## 📅 Project Milestones & Submission Deadlines

| Milestone | Week | Key Deliverables | Verification Checkpoint |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| **M1: Area Delineation & Data Sourcing** | Week 4 | Georeferenced Survey of India toposheet, downloaded satellite imagery, and project study area boundary GeoPackage. | Verified CRS (EPSG:4326 & UTM), RMS error $< 1.0$ px. |
| **M2: Vector Digitization & Base Layers** | Week 8 | Base thematic layers (roads, water bodies, administrative boundary, infrastructure). | Clean topology, zero slivers/overlaps, structured attribute table. |
| **M3: Spatial Analysis & Modeling** | Week 12 | DEM hydrological processing, spectral indices (NDVI/NDWI), or AHP multi-criteria overlay model. | Mathematical logic documented, intermediate raster rasters verified. |
| **M4: Final Geopackage & Poster Presentation** | Week 15 | Unified GeoPackage (`.gpkg`), high-res A1 print layout PDF, executive technical report, and oral viva. | Map cartography standards: scale, legend, north arrow, projection citation. |
