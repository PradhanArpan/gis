2. **Chang, Kang-tsung** (2019). *Introduction to Geographic Information Systems*, McGraw-Hill Education, 9th Edition.
3. **Bhatta, Basudeb** (2021). *Remote Sensing and GIS*, Oxford University Press, 3rd Edition.
4. **Lillesand, T., Kiefer, R. W., and Chipman, J.** (2015). *Remote Sensing and Image Interpretation*, John Wiley & Sons, 7th Edition.

## 🏛️ Standards, Guidelines & Codebooks

- **Survey of India (SOI)**: *Guidelines for Map Users on Open Series Maps (OSM)*, Department of Science & Technology, Government of India.
- **National Spatial Data Infrastructure (NSDI)**: *Metadata Standards for Geographic Information in India*, DST, New Delhi.
- **IS 14927 (Part 1 & 2): 2001**: *Geographical Information Systems — Spatial Data Formats and Transfer Standards*, Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).
- **OGC (Open Geospatial Consortium)**: *OGC GeoPackage Encoding Standard (v1.3)* and *OGC Web Map Service (WMS) Implementation Standard*.
